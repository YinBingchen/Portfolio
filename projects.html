Gene Expression Classification

Python Pandas Scikit-learn Matplotlib

I developed a Random Forest classifier with 700 decision trees to distinguish five cancer types using RNA-Seq gene expression data. I examined the distribution of cancer types and built a preprocessing pipeline with median imputation to handle missing values. I used a stratified 80/20 train-test split to preserve class proportions when training and evaluating the model.

The model correctly classified every sample in the held-out test set, achieving 100% accuracy. I used precision, recall, and a confusion matrix to evaluate the predictions across the five classes. I also discussed why these results on a controlled dataset do not establish clinical performance and why testing on more diverse data would be necessary.

Housing Price Prediction

Python Pandas Scikit-learn Matplotlib

I developed a histogram-based gradient boosting regressor to predict housing sale prices in Ames, Iowa, using property characteristics such as living area, overall quality, age, and neighborhood. I explored 1,875 training records and built a preprocessing pipeline to handle missing numeric and categorical values and encode categorical features.

On the test data, the model achieved a mean absolute percentage error of 8.54%, with 93.39% of predictions falling within 20% of the actual sale price. I compared predicted and actual prices to examine errors and discussed the limitations of applying a model trained on historical sales from one city to other locations and market conditions.

Baseball Swing Probability

Python Pandas Scikit-learn Matplotlib

I developed a histogram-based gradient boosting classifier to estimate the probability of a batter swinging at a pitch, using Zac Gallen's 2023 MLB pitch data. I explored swing rates across pitch types and built a preprocessing pipeline to encode categorical features alongside numeric measurements such as pitch speed, movement, and location. I then applied sigmoid calibration with five-fold cross-validation to improve the model's probability estimates.

On the test data, the model achieved a Brier score of 0.1785 and an expected calibration error of 0.0495. I used a calibration plot to compare predicted probabilities with observed swing rates and examined where the estimates differed. Since the data covers only one pitcher, the results would need further evaluation before applying the model to other pitchers.

April 2026

Credit Card Fraud Detection

Python Pandas Scikit-learn Random Forest

I developed a Random Forest classifier with 200 decision trees to identify fraudulent credit card transactions using transaction amounts and anonymized features. I explored 54,276 training transactions, compared transaction amounts across fraudulent and legitimate purchases, and examined the class imbalance, with fraud representing only 0.58% of the training data.

On 13,569 test transactions, the model achieved 98.48% precision, 82.28% recall, and an F1 score of 0.8966 for fraud detection. I evaluated the trade-off between false alarms and missed fraud, finding that predictions of fraud were highly reliable, but approximately 18% of fraudulent transactions went undetected.
